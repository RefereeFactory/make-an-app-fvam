Behind the scenes

How this app works

  • Buying potatoes: sacks and prices live in the farm's Factory account. Pay buttons use the Factory's secure checkout — the app never touches your card.
  • Delivery check: your postcode is geocoded by the Factory places service and the distance to the farm is measured to tell you your zone.
  • Newsletter: sign-ups are saved to permanent Factory storage with your consent choice. Only people who ticked the box go into the marketing export.
  • Owner desk: the farm sets a private code on first use to view sign-ups and download a CSV for their records.
